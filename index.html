Connecting citizens and officials for a better Bakakeng North.

NorteLink is the official mobile application created for the residents of Barangay Bakakeng North.

It is developed to help community members easily connect with their barangay hall and report local concerns.

Through the app, community members can quickly report everyday local concerns, such as uncollected garbage, damaged roads, broken streetlights, or public nuisances, along with photos and details. Beyond reporting issues, NorteLink keeps users informed with official, real-time barangay advisories, weather updates, and community notices so residents never miss important news. It also provides instant, one-tap access to local emergency hotlines and essential public service contacts, ensuring that help is always within reach when needed most.

By bridging technology and community service, NorteLink empowers residents to take an active role in improving their neighborhood. Together, the community and local officials are building a more connected, informed, and peaceful Barangay Bakakeng North with every report and update.
